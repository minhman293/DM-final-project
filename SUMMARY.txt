- Training data: most recent 391 weeks per region (truncation chosen

ENSEMBLE — HORIZON_AGGRESSIVE (FINAL SUBMISSION)

Step 1: Pre-blend deep learning components
  DL_blend = 0.64 × TFT_predictions + 0.36 × LSTM_predictions
  (This intermediate is the SMOOTHED_64TFT_36LSTM submission, 0.8189 alone)

Step 2: Horizon-dependent blending with LightGBM
  For horizon h in {1,2,3,4,5}, final prediction =
    w_DL[h] × DL_blend[h] + (1 - w_DL[h]) × LGBM[h]
  where w_DL = [0.50, 0.60, 0.70, 0.78, 0.85]

  | Horizon | DL_blend weight | LightGBM weight |
  |---------|-----------------|-----------------|
  | Week 1  | 0.50            | 0.50            |
  | Week 2  | 0.60            | 0.40            |
  | Week 3  | 0.70            | 0.30            |
  | Week 4  | 0.78            | 0.22            |
  | Week 5  | 0.85            | 0.15            |

  Justification: LightGBM has strong signal from explicit lag features at
  short horizons (lag-1 autocorrelation = 0.936); deep sequence models
  excel at long horizons via learned temporal representations.

Step 3: Temporal smoothing (3-tap)
  For h in {2,3,4}: smoothed[h] = 0.8×pred[h] + 0.1×pred[h-1] + 0.1×pred[h+1]
  Endpoint h=1: smoothed[1] = 0.8×pred[1] + 0.2×pred[2]
  Endpoint h=5: smoothed[5] = 0.8×pred[5] + 0.2×pred[4]

Step 4: Constraints
  - Clip to valid score range [0, 5]
  - Snap near-zero predictions: if pred < 0.05 then pred = 0

KEY FINDINGS FOR DISCUSSION

1. Truncation paradox: Pure TFT on full data (0.9296) was 0.04 worse than
   on truncated 391-week data (0.8891). Evidence that training-test
   covariate shift dominates over training-set size for this task.

2. Architecture-feature asymmetry: Identical climatological anomaly
   features improved LightGBM by 0.13 MAE (1.09 → 0.84) but degraded
   TFT by 0.18 MAE (0.89 → 1.07). Hypothesis: TFT's variable selection
   network already implicitly encodes seasonal patterns through its month
   embedding and gated residual layers; adding redundant explicit signal
   disrupts the learned variable selection weights. Tree splits cannot
   extract these patterns from raw weather alone, so explicit anomaly
   features are necessary.

3. Cross-family ensembling: Uniform 70/30 blend of (TFT+LSTM) and LightGBM
   improved over best deep-only blend by 0.005 MAE. Horizon-dependent
   weighting added a further 0.0013 MAE improvement. The benefit scales
   with how aggressively short-horizon predictions favor LightGBM,
   consistent with the underlying signal-decay structure.

4. Median is MAE-optimal: Shifting the TFT prediction from q=0.5 to q=0.25
   degraded MAE by 0.05. Confirms theoretical result that median minimizes
   MAE for the conditional distribution of drought scores.

5. Public-LB noise floor: Five blend-weight variants of TFT+LSTM clustered
   within 0.008 MAE (0.8273 to 0.8356). This sampling noise informed our
   decision to stop tuning blend weights and switch to architectural
   diversification instead.

LIMITATIONS

- Did not exceed Baseline 3 (0.8056); final 0.8129 is in the upper portion
  of the "between B2 and B3" grade band.
- Local validation set (last 60 weeks per region) did not perfectly proxy
  Kaggle test distribution — evidence: q=0.25 experiment improved local
  val but regressed on Kaggle.
- Did not explore recursive multi-step forecasting (using week-h prediction
  as feature for week-(h+1)); could improve long-horizon predictions.
- LightGBM bias toward overestimation visible from MEGA_BLEND failures
  with heavier LGBM weighting; principled calibration not attempted.
